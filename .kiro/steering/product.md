# HyperCrypt — Product Steering

## Product Vision
HyperCrypt resurrects **1987 Apple HyperCard & HyperTalk** for the modern web—bridging the gap between static Markdown documents and heavy cloud-dependent web apps. Users play and author 512×342 1-bit interactive card stacks in the browser, write plain-English HyperTalk scripts with a live step debugger, export any stack as a single immortal offline `.html` file, and compile Markdown runbooks into stacks from Kiro IDE via `hypercrypt-mcp`.

## Core Product Principles
1. **Zero Empty-State Friction**: First load opens directly into **Browse Mode** on Card 1 of *The Attic Mac of 1987*. Never greet visitors with a blank canvas or login wall.
2. **Warm Vintage × Playful Kiroween Aesthetic**: Two skins—**1987 Cream** (warm archival paper `#FBF8F1`, charcoal ink `#24201B`, vintage amber `#D96B27`) and **Kiroween Dusk** (cozy twilight violet `#21193A`, moonlight ink `#F3EEFF`, playful pumpkin `#FF9E44`). Keep the tone charming, tactile, and nostalgic—never grim horror.
3. **Real Practical Utility Alongside Delight**: Ship 3 flagship built-in stacks:
   - *The Attic Mac of 1987* (6-card playful mystery & live tour)
   - *Prod 502 Triage Runbook* (5-card practical SRE decision deck compiled from Markdown)
   - *Atkinson Darkroom & Script Lab* (4-card 1-bit dithering, retro audio & step-debugger lab)
4. **Immortal Portability**: Every stack exports cleanly to a standalone offline `.html` file with zero external CDN dependencies.
